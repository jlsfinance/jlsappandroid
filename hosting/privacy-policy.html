Privacy Policy — JLS Suite

Last updated: July 18, 2026  ·  Owner: JLS Suite Ltd  ·  Contact: lovneetrathi@gmail.com

Non-Lending Entity Declaration: JLS Suite is purely a management and record-keeping tool for businesses. We are NOT a lender, bank, NBFC, or financial institution. We do not provide loans, process payments to third parties, or connect users with lenders. The app is used by business owners to maintain their own private books of accounts (loans given, deposits collected, customer records).

1. Introduction

JLS Suite ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information that you enter into the app for your own business records, including:

  • Account information (name, email, business ID) for authentication.
  • Business ledger data (customer names, contact details, loan/deposit records, repayment schedules, notes, and optional customer photos you choose to attach).

We do not collect or access Contacts, Call Logs, SMS, or precise location from your device.

3. Permissions Used

  • Camera: Only when you choose to capture a customer photo or document for your own records.
  • Storage / Files: To save and export PDF reports and receipts you generate.
  • Notifications: For ledger updates and scheduled reminders you configure.

4. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store and calculate your business ledger (loans, EMIs, deposits).
  • Generate reports and receipts you request.
  • Send in-app reminders you schedule.

5. Data Sharing

We follow a strict Zero Data Sharing policy. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to outside parties, credit bureaus, or recovery agents. Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase infrastructure and is accessible only by you and users you explicitly authorize.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards (Firebase Authentication, Firestore Security Rules, encrypted transit) to protect your information.

7. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app. This removes your login credentials and personal profile immediately.

Business ledger records (loans, deposits, customer entries) are retained as required by applicable financial record-keeping regulations and are disassociated from your deleted login. To request removal of specific business records, contact us at lovneetrathi@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly solicit information from or market to children under 13.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy questions or data requests, email lovneetrathi@gmail.com.
