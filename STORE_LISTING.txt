Welcome to Loan Book & Debt Manager — the #1 smart ledger for finance businesses! Say goodbye to book-keeping headaches and save time managing your loans, EMIs, and deposits — all from one secure app, at zero extra cost.

🔐 Securely manage your lending and deposit business and keep your data safe with Loan Book & Debt Manager. Your records are stored privately on Google Firebase and accessible only by you and the team you authorize.

👥 Collaborate with your team on Loan Book & Debt Manager and streamline your daily collections and deposit operations.

📈 Keep track of your loans, EMIs, and deposits with ease — see exactly what is due, overdue, or collected.

💰 Manage multiple businesses in one place and stay organized, whether you run loans, RD/FD deposits, or both.

📊 Export your data in PDF or Excel formats, and analyze your business performance from clear dashboards.

🏦 Deposits made simple: open RD/FD deposits, track every kist, handle maturities, and even foreclose early with a transparent charge and a downloadable certificate.

📲 Auto Reminders: set up WhatsApp reminders for your borrowers and depositors — they are sent so you never lose track of a due date.

WHO IS IT FOR?
Finance companies, deposit collectors, agents, and small lenders who need a reliable mobile ledger without complexity or recurring software cost.

KEY FEATURES
• Loan Management — create loans, set EMIs, track schedules, monitor overdue.
• EMI Collection — collect on the go, auto-generate receipts.
• Deposit Tracking — RD/FD management, kist collection, maturity payouts.
• Foreclosure — early close with clear charges + certificate (with photo).
• Customer Profiles — details, photos, KYC (Aadhaar/PAN), guarantor info.
• Due Lists & Reminders — overdue + monthly due, one-tap WhatsApp reminders.
• Reports & Finance — dashboards, cash-flow, partner accounts, PDF/Excel export.
• Multi-Company — switch businesses from one account.
• Secure — Firebase auth with per-company data isolation.
